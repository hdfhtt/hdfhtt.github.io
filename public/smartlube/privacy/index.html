SmartLube Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 November 2026

SmartLube (“the app”) is the companion app for a SmartLube chain oiler fitted to a motorbike. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app.

The short version

The app has no account, no sign-up and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit or sell your personal information. Everything the app remembers is stored on your own phone, and the only server it ever contacts is GitHub, to look for firmware updates for your oiler.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The names you give your oilers, for example “Kawasaki W175”.
  • The identifier each oiler broadcasts, which is derived from its hardware and is used to recognise the same unit next time.
  • Which oiler you connected to last, so the app can reconnect to it.

This is held in your phone’s own app storage (Android SharedPreferences, iOS UserDefaults). It never leaves your device by way of the app. If you have Android backup enabled for your Google account, your phone’s operating system may include this app’s stored data in your personal device backup; that backup belongs to you and is governed by Google’s terms, not by us.

Your oiler’s settings — power state, boost mode and flow duration — are stored on the oiler itself, not by the app.

Bluetooth and location permissions

The app uses Bluetooth to find and control your oiler. Android requires the “Nearby devices” permission (BLUETOOTH_SCAN and BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) for this.

On Android 11 and older, the operating system additionally requires the location permission before any app is allowed to scan for Bluetooth devices. The app asks for it only for that reason. It does not use, derive, store or transmit your location, and it has no location features.

Bluetooth is used only to communicate with your oiler. No data gathered over Bluetooth is sent anywhere.

Internet access

The app connects to the internet for one purpose: checking whether newer firmware has been published for your oiler, and downloading it when you choose to install it. These requests go to GitHub, where the firmware releases are published.

As with any web request, GitHub receives the technical information needed to answer it, including your IP address. That information is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement. We do not receive it, and we operate no server of our own.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or store personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not require an account.

Children

The app is not directed to children. It is a tool for owners of a motorbike chain oiler and has no features of interest to children.

Removing your data

Hold a saved device in the app’s device picker and choose Forget to remove that oiler and its name. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. If your phone backs up app data to your Google account, remove it through your Google account’s backup settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at https://hdfhtt.github.io/smartlube/privacy/ with a new date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
